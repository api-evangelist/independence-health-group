# Independence Health Group (independence-health-group)

Independence Health Group is the Philadelphia-headquartered parent holding company of Independence Blue Cross (the Blue Cross and Blue Shield licensee for southeastern Pennsylvania), AmeriHealth Administrators (a national third-party benefits administrator), and AmeriHealth New Jersey. Through its subsidiaries the group serves approximately seven million members and is one of the largest health insurers in the Philadelphia region. Independence Health Group itself does not publish a developer portal or APIs; all CMS Interoperability and Patient Access surfaces are operated by the subsidiary plans (notably Independence Blue Cross and AmeriHealth New Jersey).
